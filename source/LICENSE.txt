ILLUSTRATIVE EXAMPLES (EXPLANATORY ONLY; NOT ADDITIONAL TERMS)
======================================================================

These examples explain the proposed clauses; they do not prove ownership or replace the completed agreement.

1. Ownership: if the verified Licensor owns the original code, that ownership is not transferred merely because the Buyer receives a ZIP. Any third-party material remains owned by its respective rights holder.
2. Grant: after an effective signed agreement, the Buyer may operate one named storefront and add products/prices for that store.
3. Restrictions: the Buyer may not upload the source ZIP to a public repository or sell copies as a reusable website template without written permission.
4. Intellectual property: the Buyer must not remove a required third-party license notice from a dependency or imply that a third-party logo belongs to the Licensor.
5. Support: if the order expressly includes 30-day email support beginning on a stated date, reproducible defects in the agreed scope may be reported by email; phone and 24/7 support are not implied.
6. Warranty: a configuration that works on one tested Node/Cloudflare environment is not a promise it will work in every host or every future version.
7. Liability: a proposed cap must be completed and checked by counsel; it cannot override a liability that applicable law does not allow the parties to limit.
8. Indemnification: any indemnity must be limited to defined claims and fair procedures; it does not automatically shift every claim to the Buyer or remove mandatory rights.
9. Third-party rights: an npm dependency may carry its own license and notice requirements; the Buyer must not assume this proprietary draft overrides that license.
10. Termination: if a material breach is capable of remedy, the final agreement may give written notice and a cure period before termination, subject to applicable law.
11. Governing law: the parties must complete the law and forum after reviewing seller/buyer location and any mandatory consumer rules; a list of example countries is not a complete clause.
12. Amendments: adding a second production storefront or payment integration should be documented in a signed written change order before that work/use begins.
