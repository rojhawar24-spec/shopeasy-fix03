# Legal drafting sources and limitations

Reviewed on 16 October 2026. These official sources support general statements in the templates; they do not replace advice for the seller's actual facts and jurisdiction.

- GDPR official text, including Articles 28 and 33: https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679
- EU consumer online shopping/withdrawal overview: https://europa.eu/youreurope/citizens/consumers/shopping/shopping-consumer-rights/index_en.htm
- EU returns and exceptions: https://europa.eu/youreurope/citizens/consumers/shopping/returns/index_en.htm
- Belgian FPS Economy guidance on unfair contract terms: https://economie.fgov.be/nl/themas/verkoop/contracten/onrechtmatige-bedingen
- California Privacy Protection Agency: https://cppa.ca.gov/ (check current applicability and regulations)

Legal caveats: country rules vary by seller location, buyer location, product type, business/consumer status, cross-border targeting and deployment. No template guarantees enforceability or compliance. Liability caps, indemnities, consumer rights, governing-law/forum clauses, retention periods and transfer clauses need jurisdiction-specific legal review. The 14-day EU withdrawal right has exceptions and must not be copied as an unconditional rule for every product or buyer. No country-specific return period is asserted here without current verification from an authoritative source.
