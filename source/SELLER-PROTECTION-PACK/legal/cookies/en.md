## What storage is used?
The current ShopEasy source uses session/security cookies and a `shop_lang` language-preference cookie. Verify exact session cookie names and attributes in the deployed environment. The language cookie is currently set for up to one year and is not an advertising cookie. Hosting/CDN/security providers may set additional items; inventory them before publication.

## Categories
- **Strictly necessary:** session, CSRF/security and load-balancing items that are required for requested functions. Explain each actual cookie and expiry.
- **Preferences:** language selection (`shop_lang`) is a preference. Decide whether local law requires consent for this specific use and provide a clear way to change it.
- **Analytics / marketing:** no analytics or marketing provider is included by default in this package. Do not add scripts or claim these categories are active unless separately integrated. Where consent is required, block them until the user chooses.

## Controls
Browser controls can delete or block cookies, but blocking necessary cookies may prevent login, checkout or security features. If non-essential cookies are introduced, implement and test a banner with equally prominent **Accept**, **Reject** and **Settings** choices, granular categories, no preselected optional categories, withdrawal/change of consent, and a consent record where required. A written policy alone is not a consent-management system.
