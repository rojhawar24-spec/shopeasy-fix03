# Data Processing Agreement (DPA) — conditional template

**Use only if the software seller actually processes personal data on behalf of the store.** If the seller only supplies code and cannot access production data, a processor DPA may not describe the relationship correctly. Have counsel assess roles before signature.

Parties: Controller [STORE LEGAL NAME, ADDRESS] and Processor [SUPPLIER LEGAL NAME, ADDRESS]. Effective date: [DATE]. Main contract: [REFERENCE].

## 1. Scope and duration
Subject matter: [specific support/hosting/maintenance activity]. Duration: [term]. Nature and purpose: [documented instructions]. Data subjects: [customers/staff/etc.]. Data categories: [actual categories]. Special-category data: [none unless documented and lawful].

## 2. Instructions and confidentiality
Processor processes personal data only on documented instructions from Controller, including transfers, unless law requires otherwise. Persons authorised to process data are bound by confidentiality. No processing activity is implied by delivery of source code alone.

## 3. Security
Processor implements appropriate technical and organisational measures proportionate to risk, described in an annex: access control, least privilege, secure transfer, secrets management, patching, backup and incident response. Do not sign until the measures actually used are documented.

## 4. Subprocessors and transfers
Approved subprocessors: [LIST / NONE]. Give prior notice of changes and a reasonable objection process. Identify transfer locations and lawful transfer safeguards: [DETAILS].

## 5. Assistance and rights requests
Taking account of processing, Processor assists Controller through appropriate technical/organisational measures with data-subject requests and Controller obligations, to the extent feasible and agreed. Fees for extraordinary assistance: [TERMS].

## 6. Personal-data incidents
Processor notifies Controller without undue delay after becoming aware of a personal-data breach and supplies available facts as they become known. The Controller determines legal notifications to authorities and affected people. Do not replace this with an unconditional promise that the processor alone will notify the regulator within 72 hours.

## 7. Deletion, return and audit
At end of services, Processor deletes or returns personal data as instructed, unless law requires retention, and explains backups/log retention. Audit rights, notice, scope, confidentiality and costs: [REASONABLE TERMS]. A 30-day notice period must not prevent urgent regulatory or security investigations.

## 8. Liability and conflicts
Liability allocation is subject to mandatory law and the main contract. No clause removes rights or duties that cannot legally be excluded. If terms conflict, [ORDER OF PRECEDENCE].
