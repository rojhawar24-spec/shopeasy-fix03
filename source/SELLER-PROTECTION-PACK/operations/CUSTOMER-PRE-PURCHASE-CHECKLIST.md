# Customer pre-purchase checklist

Before paying, the customer should understand:

1. This is a configurable development template, not a hosted, fully finished store.
2. They need a domain, hosting/deployment account, Turso/database account and ongoing service budget.
3. They must supply accurate products, photos, prices, stock, legal business identity, contact details and branding.
4. They must write/review store terms, privacy notice, cookie policy, shipping/returns/refunds and complaint process for their business and countries.
5. Current default payment method is COD. No online payment gateway is included by default.
6. No courier integration, country-specific VAT/tax engine, or automatic international tax calculation is included by default.
7. The UI supports English, Sorani Kurdish, Arabic and Dutch (Belgium); legal templates and merchant policy text must still be checked separately in the customer's actual market.
8. The intended market must be configured explicitly; deployment, delivery coverage, local law, import rules, product safety and payment processes require validation for that market.
9. GDPR/CCPA compliance is not guaranteed. The customer needs a real data map, legal basis, retention schedule, request handling and applicable consent mechanisms.
10. Admin 2FA, full browser testing and external penetration testing have not been certified.
11. Support, bug-fix definition, start date, working hours, response target and exclusions must appear in the signed quote.
12. Third-party libraries/assets have their own licenses; the seller must confirm its authority to grant the proposed license before the customer relies on it.
13. Refund/cancellation rights cannot be removed where mandatory law applies.
14. The SHA-256 must be computed from the exact delivered file, not copied from a previous ZIP.
