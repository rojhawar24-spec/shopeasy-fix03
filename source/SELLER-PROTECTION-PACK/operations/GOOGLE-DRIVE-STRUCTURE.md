# Client evidence folder — create manually in your own Google Drive

`Client_[CLIENT-LEGAL-NAME]_[YYYY-MM-DD]/`

1. `01_WhatsApp_Conversation.pdf` — export relevant consent/quote; avoid unnecessary personal data
2. `02_Email_Confirmation.pdf` — final scope and price
3. `03_Payment_Proof.pdf` — redact bank/account details not needed for evidence
4. `04_LICENSE_SIGNED.pdf` — final executed license, not the draft
5. `05_PURCHASE_AGREEMENT_SIGNED.pdf`
6. `06_DPA_SIGNED_IF_APPLICABLE.pdf` — only if seller actually processes personal data
7. `07_ZIP_SHA256.txt` — hash of the exact delivered ZIP
8. `08_DELIVERY_CONFIRMATION.pdf`
9. `09_SETUP_GUIDE.pdf`
10. `10_SUPPORT_LOG.txt` — date, issue ID, summary, action; no passwords or customer personal data
11. `11_DEPENDENCY_LICENSE_NOTICES/` — third-party notices and inventory
12. `12_TEST_REPORT.pdf` — exact commands, environment, passed/failed/blocked results

Permissions: private by default, seller account only. Share with the client only the files they need. Enable MFA, avoid public links, review access periodically, and delete records under a documented retention schedule. This document does not create the folder or configure Google Drive permissions.
